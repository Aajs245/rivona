Architecture

Rivona Architects is a premier design studio rooted in Mangaluru, crafting architectural experiences that transcend the ordinary. We believe every space has a soul — our work is to reveal it.

From luxury residences to experiential commercial interiors, we bring an uncompromising eye for detail and an obsession with the interplay of light, material, and human emotion.
